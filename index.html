Nordic Transit Replay — How to use

What is this?
Nordic Transit Replay captures 24 hours of real public-transport movement across four Nordic capitals — Helsinki, Stockholm, Oslo, and Reykjavik — and replays it as an animated dark map. Vehicle positions were recorded every 30–120 seconds from live feeds, giving roughly 720–2 880 frames per city per day.
Choosing a city
🗺
Click any city card to open its individual replay player. Each player loads only that city's data (~7–80 MB) and lets you explore it in detail.
⊞
Use All cities synced ↗ in the footer to watch all four cities play simultaneously from a single shared timeline — great for side-by-side comparison.
Dates
📅
Two recordings are available: Tuesday 8 April and Tuesday 14 April 2026. Select the date inside each city player using the pill buttons in the right panel. Helsinki also has Sunday 15 Mar and Monday 16 Mar for weekday comparison.
Playback controls
⏮⏭
Jump to start / end — skip to midnight or the last snapshot.
◀ ▶
Step — move one snapshot backward or forward.
▶⏸
Play / Pause — start or stop automatic playback at the selected speed.
━●━
Scrubber — drag to jump to any moment instantly.
1× = real time  ·  10× = one tick every 3 s (default)  ·  60× = two ticks per second — full 24 h in ~24 min

Corridor trails (🖌 Paint)
🖌
Paint — permanently dot-paints every vehicle position onto the map as playback advances, revealing the full network geometry over time.
🧹
Clear — wipes the painted canvas so you can start fresh.
Tip: enable Paint, jump to 07:00 and run at 30× — watch the morning rush build across the network.
Timezones in the synced view
All four cities play from the same UTC clock. Local times differ by design — Helsinki noon (UTC+3) = Stockholm 11:00 (UTC+2) = Reykjavik 09:00 (UTC+0). Each city badge shows its UTC offset for reference.
